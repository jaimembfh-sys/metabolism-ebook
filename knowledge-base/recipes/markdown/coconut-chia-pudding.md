## Ingredients

### Make the Pudding
- 1/2 cup chia seeds
- 2 cups canned coconut milk
- 1 1/2 Tbsp monk fruit/allulose sweetener <!-- linked_product: url=null -->
- 1/2 tsp real vanilla extract
- Pinch of sea salt

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Pudding
In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the chia seeds, coconut milk, sweetener, vanilla, and salt until combined. Divide the mixture among 4 jars or cereal bowls and stir once more.

### 2. Chill
Cover the chia puddings and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, until thickened. For the smoothest texture, stir or shake each jar every 5 minutes for the first 20 minutes so the chia seeds don't clump at the bottom.

### 3. Serve
Once thickened, top with your favorite toppings — fresh berries, toasted coconut, or a spoonful of nut butter all work well — and enjoy.

## Notes

### Storage
Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week, or freeze in a shallow container for up to 6 months.
