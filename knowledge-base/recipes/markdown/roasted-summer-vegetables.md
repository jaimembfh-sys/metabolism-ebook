## Ingredients

### Prep the Oven and Vegetables
- 2 large zucchini or yellow summer squash, sliced into 1/2-inch thick slices
- 2 bell peppers, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, halved
- 1 red onion, cut into 1-inch wedges
- 2 large carrots, cut into 1/2-inch thick rounds or half-moons

### Season
- 2 to 3 Tbsp avocado oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- Optional: 1 tsp granulated garlic or onion powder, 1 tsp dried herbs (oregano, rosemary, thyme, or Italian seasoning), 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

### Serve
- Fresh herb sprigs, for garnish, optional

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the Oven and Vegetables
Preheat the oven to 400°F. Cut the vegetables into similar thicknesses so they roast evenly, and place them in a large bowl (either individually or all together).

### 2. Season
Drizzle the vegetables lightly with avocado oil, just enough to coat. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, and any optional seasonings, then toss gently with tongs to coat evenly.

### 3. Arrange and Roast
Place the seasoned vegetables on an extra-large parchment-lined sheet pan (or use two pans) in a single layer. Roast on the middle rack for 15 minutes.

### 4. Finish Roasting
Toss the vegetables and rotate the pan if needed. Continue roasting until fork-tender, another 10 to 15 minutes. Since carrots take a bit longer than the other vegetables, keep them cut on the thinner side, or roast them separately if you want everything to finish at the same time.

### 5. Serve
Transfer to a serving dish, garnish with fresh herbs if desired, and cover to keep warm until ready to serve.

## Nutrition

- Calories: 80
- Total Fat: 4g

## Notes

### Storage
Keeps for up to 4 days in an airtight container in the fridge, and can be reheated in the oven or air fryer. Freezes well for up to 3 months.
