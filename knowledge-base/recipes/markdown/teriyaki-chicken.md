## Ingredients

### Prep the Chicken
- 1 1/4 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts, diced into 1-inch cubes

### Make the Marinade
- 1/4 cup tamari or coconut aminos
- 1/4 cup water
- 3 1/2 Tbsp monk fruit allulose blend <!-- linked_product: url=null -->
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tsp peeled and minced fresh ginger
- 2 tsp peeled and minced fresh garlic (2 cloves)
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum <!-- linked_product: url=null -->

### Cook the Chicken
- 1 Tbsp beef tallow or avocado oil

### Serve
- Sesame seeds and chopped green onions, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the Chicken
Wash the chicken and pat it completely dry with paper towels. Dice into 1-inch cubes.

### 2. Make the Marinade
In a bowl, whisk together the tamari or coconut aminos, water, monk fruit allulose blend, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Sprinkle in the xanthan gum while whisking continuously — this keeps it from clumping. Whisk until fully smooth.

### 3. Marinate the Chicken
Add the chicken to the marinade and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, up to overnight, for the best flavor.

### 4. Cook the Chicken
Heat the beef tallow or avocado oil in a 12-inch non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Remove the chicken from the marinade with a slotted spoon, reserving the marinade, and add the chicken to the skillet. Cook, giving the pieces space, until browned on the bottom, about 3 to 4 minutes, then flip and cook until the center reaches 165°F, about 3 minutes longer.

### 5. Add the Sauce
Pour the reserved marinade into the skillet with the chicken. Cook and toss until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken, about 30 to 60 seconds.

### 6. Serve
Serve warm over cauliflower rice, garnished with sesame seeds and green onions if desired.

## Notes

### Storage
Keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 to 4 days.
