## Ingredients

### Prep the Brussels Sprouts
- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, cleaned and trimmed

### Season and Toss
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled and sliced
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 3 Tbsp avocado oil

### Roast
- Extra grated Parmesan, for serving

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the Brussels Sprouts
Preheat the oven to 400°F. Cut the Brussels sprouts in half and place them in an oven-safe dish. Make sure to dry them very well before cooking — the drier they are, the crispier they'll get.

### 2. Season and Toss
Add the garlic, Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper to the dish, followed by the avocado oil. Toss well to coat.

### 3. Roast
Roast uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, until crisp, brown, and caramelized on the outside and tender on the inside. Serve topped with more grated Parmesan.

## Notes

### Storage
Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in the oven at 350°F until warmed through, about 5 to 7 minutes, or in the microwave.
