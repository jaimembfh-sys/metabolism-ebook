## Ingredients

### Build the Lettuce Base
- 6 cups chopped green leaf or romaine lettuce (or a mix of both)

### Arrange the Toppings in Rows
- 2 cups cubed rotisserie chicken
- 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (or 1/3 cup bacon bits)
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1 cup cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese (or shredded cheddar)
- 1/4 cup pickled red onion

### Make the Vinaigrette
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Pinch of sugar (optional)

## Instructions

### 1. Build the Lettuce Base
Spread the lettuce in a large bowl or on a large platter.

### 2. Arrange the Toppings in Rows
Arrange each ingredient in its own row or section on top of the lettuce, so everything is visible.

### 3. Make the Vinaigrette
Combine the vinegar, mustard, salt, pepper, and sugar in a jar with a tight lid. Add the olive oil, screw on the lid, and shake vigorously until emulsified.

### 4. Dress and Serve
Drizzle the vinaigrette over the salad, or serve it on the side so everyone can add their own. Toss just before eating.

## Nutrition

### Per serving (makes about 4 servings)
- Calories: 663
- Total Fat: 55g
- Total Carbs: 9g
- Fiber: 5g
- Net Carbs: 4g
- Protein: 34g

## Notes

### Storage
Store components separately in airtight containers for up to 3 days. Wait to dice the avocado and add dressing until just before serving.
