SUPPLEMENTS ARE A GUESSING GAME. YOUR BODY PAYS FOR IT.

You're tired, foggy, cold, achy, or just not right. So you guess. Another bottle, another protocol, another post that swears this is the one.

Guessing has a cost:

  • Too much of one nutrient can push another one out. High-dose zinc drains copper. Calcium blocks iron.
  • Many synthetic forms aren't what your body actually uses. Your system has to convert them, and not everyone converts well.
  • And the nutrient you actually need may not be in anything you're taking.

"NORMAL" LABS DON'T MEAN NORMAL CELLS.

Blood work is a snapshot of what's in your bloodstream at one moment, not what's happening inside your cells.

Your body protects blood levels at all costs, pulling from your tissues to keep the number steady. Only about 1% of your magnesium is in your blood. Your levels can look fine while your cells run short.
