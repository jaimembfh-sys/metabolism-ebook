YOU EAT WELL, YOU TAKE THE SUPPLEMENTS, AND YOU STILL DON'T FEEL RIGHT.

Your symptoms are real. Your body is telling you something is missing.

You're tired no matter how much you sleep. Your hair is thinning, your hands are cold, your mood is off, or your body just won't respond the way it used to. You bring it up and you're told your labs are "normal," or that it's stress, or age.

So you start guessing. A bottle of magnesium here, a B-complex there, whatever the latest post said to take. Some of it might help. Some of it might be the wrong form, the wrong dose, or something your medication is quietly cancelling out.

Your symptoms aren't random. Fatigue, brain fog, muscle cramps, poor sleep and slow healing all have biology behind them, and very often that biology is a nutrient your body isn't getting, isn't absorbing, or is using up faster than you're replacing it.

BioNut AI connects your symptoms, your diet, your supplements and your medications to the physiology underneath, and shows you which nutrients are most likely low and exactly which foods fill the gap.
