MetaBurn — three design directions

Same four screens in each: Today, Coach, My Protocol and Meal Planner. Same content throughout, so what differs is the design. Open each on your phone and your laptop — the navigation changes from a bottom tab bar to a side rail at 900px.

A · Clean & minimal

Quiet and flat. Hairline borders, lots of air, gold used sparingly as an accent. Serif reserved for screen titles and numbers.

--ink #1f2e35

B · Warm & editorial

Cream paper, gold rules, italic serif headings and a drop cap in the rationale. Closest to the book the app came from.

--ink #2c3e50

C · Modern health app

Dark chrome, rounded cards on a soft grey field, pill controls, chat bubbles with a tail. Closest to Oura or Whoop.

--ink #1f2e35

Previews only — nothing here touches the real app. The chat send button and the meal-planner regenerate button both run a simulated AI delay so you can judge the loading states. Switch tabs, reload, and the app returns to the tab you were on.
