Rule of 3s Safe starches

Your starter habits

  1. Eat protein and non-starchy vegetables first at each meal, before any starches
  2. Walk 5–10 minutes after dinner, before the evening energy dip hits
  3. Three meals on a fixed schedule, with protein at breakfast within an hour of waking

Why this plan

You're not quitting after two weeks because you lack willpower — you're quitting because your nervous system is running a famine signal. Shift work, 5–6 hours of sleep and stress at 8/10 all point the same way. The Rule of 3s with safe starches sends the opposite signal: consistency, adequacy, safety.
