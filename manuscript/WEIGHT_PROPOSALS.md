# Weight proposals — ingredient lines with no stated weight

Generated by `tools/macros/weight-proposals.js` from a `report.js` dump.
**Proposals only — nothing here has been applied to `recipes.json` or any recipe.**

These are the lines where the amount is a count rather than a weight or a volume,
so the gram weight behind the macros is an assumption the tool makes rather than
something a reader can see. The proposed line adds that weight and changes nothing
else.

## What you are approving

- **44 proposals** across 23 recipes — section 1.
- **8 lines held back** where the count already carries a standard size — section 2.
- **21 lines left alone** under 30 g, where a weight would be noise — section 3.

Applying a proposal **changes the macros**. A weight in brackets after the food
name becomes the line total, beating the count-times-typical-weight the tool
infers now, so a rounded amount moves the number. The recipes are written in whole
ounces and quarter pounds — there is no gram and no half ounce anywhere in the
book — and a 70 g onion cannot be said in whole ounces without moving 19%.

That sounds worse than it is, because the lines that round badly are the ones that
carry almost no calories. Costed through to the plate:

- Largest drift on any single line: **-19%** (1 small onion, finely chopped).
- Recipes whose published per-serving numbers would move at all: **16 of 23**.
- Recipes moving by 5 kcal or more per serving: **0**.

Full projection in section 4.
