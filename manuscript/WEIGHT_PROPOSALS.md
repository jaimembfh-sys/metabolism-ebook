# Weight proposals — ingredient lines with no stated weight

Generated by `tools/macros/weight-proposals.js` from a `report.js` dump.

This tool finds the lines where the amount is a count rather than a weight or a
volume, so the gram weight behind the macros is an assumption it makes rather than
something a reader can see, and proposes a line that states it.

## Nothing outstanding

**No ingredient line is left where the amount is a bare count.** Every line over
the 30 g floor now states a weight, so the gram figure behind each recipe's macros
is one a reader can see and check rather than one this tool assumed.

What remains below are the two standing decisions, unchanged:

- **8 lines held back** where the count already carries a standard size — section 2.
- **21 lines left alone** under 30 g, where a weight would be noise — section 3.

Re-run this after editing any recipe: if a new count-only line appears, it shows up
here as a proposal with its cost to the published numbers.
