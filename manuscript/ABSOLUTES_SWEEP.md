## Method

Flags a sentence when an absolute quantifier or superlative appears alongside a
physiological mechanism claim. Excludes pure advice phrasing ("never combine heavy
carbohydrates with heavy fats") where the absolute is an instruction, not a claim
about how the body works.

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 — absolute claim in a pulled-out element** (Key Insight, pull quote,
  summary bullet). Highest exposure: these are what a skeptical reader reads first,
  and what gets screenshotted.
- **Tier 2 — absolute claim in body text.** Same category of overstatement, lower
  visibility.
- **Tier 3 — absolute in advice or rhetorical framing.** Usually defensible; listed
  for completeness, not as a defect.
