FOR THOSE WHO REALLY STRUGGLE, I KNOW WHAT IT'S LIKE.

I'm not your typical 30-year-old healthy fitness coach who puts a little effort in and instantly gets results. I became very sick at 15 and it turned into severe chronic fatigue. At the time, despite eating mostly whole foods and doing all the sports — which I forced myself to continue despite debilitating fatigue — the weight packed on.

Over the years I ate less and less and it became harder and harder. As my chronic conditions progressed, exercise wasn't always an option, and if I ate too little it made my fatigue worse. I felt like I had tried it all and it just got harder.

I've wanted to give up many times and resign to poor health and letting myself become overweight, and at times I did to a small degree. But giving up isn't in my nature, so instead I dug in. I found the experts. I learned to spot proper research from the junk.

I compiled this information for myself and now I'm giving it to you. The straight-up truth, what the lies are, and the nuanced details that matter for someone in their mid-40s dealing with weight loss resistance and perimenopause. Everything you need to understand so you stop falling for clickbait and expensive plans that don't work.
